//? Prisma Workflow

  1. Define Schema
    Write your models in schema.prisma
  2. Run Migrate
    Prisma creates your database tables
  3. Generate Client
    Prisma builds a typed client for you
  4. Write Queries
    Use prisma.model.method() in your app
  5. Iterate
    Change schema, migrate again, repeat
